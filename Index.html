About Daugavpils

City Facts

  • Population: Approximately 78,850
  • Region: Latgale Region
  • Founded: 1275
  • Geographic Location: Eastern Latvia, near the Daugava River

Historical Highlights

Daugavpils is the second-largest city in Latvia, known for its historical fortresses and vibrant cultural scene. It plays a significant role in the country's history and development.
